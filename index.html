Recent checks
How this works, and what it can't do
  • A body-tracking model (MoveNet, run on your phone with TensorFlow.js) estimates where your shoulders, elbows, hips, knees and ankles are in the picture.
  • For a video it looks at frames of the clip and picks the one that matters most for the exercise, such as the bottom of a squat.
  • Then it applies a few simple rules about angles and positions. They are rough, commonly used coaching cues, not a clinical assessment.
  • The model itself is downloaded when you analyze, so that step may need a connection. Your photo or video stays on your phone.
  • It can't see bar path, tempo, how hard the set was or what you feel. Each check only makes sense from the camera angle it names.
  • Each check you run is saved here with the joints drawn on it, not your original photo or video.
